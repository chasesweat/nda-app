01
🔐
Logging In
One-tap Face ID or fingerprint after your first password login.
Free for all drivers
1
Select your name & enter password
Pick your name from the list and type your password on first use.
2
Set up Face ID or fingerprint
A prompt appears right after first login. Tap Set Up — from then on it's one tap. Manage it any time from Team → your driver card → 🔐 Biometric.
3
Stay Logged In
Check this on your personal phone so the app remembers you between sessions.
02
🏠
Dashboard
Your real-time home base — rides, confirmations, and earnings at a glance.
Rides — FreeEarnings — Premium ✦
1
Your upcoming rides
All rides for today and this week in time order. Tap any card to open the full details.
2
📋 Confirmations due tomorrow
Orange banner shows rides needing a day-before text. Tap to go straight to To Confirm. Requires Premium subscription.
3
💰 Monthly earnings snapshot (Premium ✦)
Gross fares, expenses, and net take-home for the current month.
4
15% Client Fee tracker (Premium ✦)
Shows fees you owe or are owed. Pay via one-tap Venmo, mark paid, or waive.
03
📝
Quoting a Ride
Build a price, text it to the client, then book it with one tap.
Free for all drivers
1
Pick your trip type
AP Round Trip, AP Run, AP Pick Up, Round Trip, One Way, or Local. The form adjusts automatically.
2
Enter addresses — mileage fills automatically
Google Maps handles the mileage. Any miles over 50 get priced automatically.
3
Add surcharges
Late Notice · Holiday · Traffic · Fuel (rounds down to a whole dollar) · Extra Stop · Wait Time.
4
Send Quote → Book This Ride
Send Quote opens Messages with the breakdown ready. Book This Ride saves the ride and prompts you to text a scheduling confirmation.
04
➕
Booking a Ride
Get a ride on the calendar in under 30 seconds with Quick Add.
Free for all drivers
1
Tap ➕ to open Quick Add
Available from the calendar or bottom nav. Client name and date are the only required fields.
2
Fill in the details
Trip type, vehicle, driver, price, client phone, flight number for airport pickups, terminal, and notes.
3
Save → two-step text prompt
Step 1: scheduling confirmation text. Step 2: booking policy text. Both pre-written — tap Send or Skip.
4
XXL double vehicle
Check Double Vehicle for large groups — adds a second driver, vehicle, and price.
Availability check
  • If the selected driver is unavailable, a popup asks you to confirm before saving. If you proceed, the driver is asked to accept or decline — and you'll hear back either way.
05
📅
Calendar
Your full schedule, color-coded by driver — week or month view.
Free for all drivers
1
Week view or Month view
Toggle at the top. Week view lays rides in time slots. Month view shows color pills per day.
2
Tap a ride for full detail
Driver, vehicle, price, navigation link, flight tracking for AP Pick Ups, TSA links for AP Runs. Contact info is only visible to the ride's driver and creator.
3
Edit, cancel, duplicate
Change any field, cancel while keeping the record, or duplicate to re-book the same client on a new date instantly.
4
🔗 Linked AP Round Trip cards
An AP Round Trip creates two linked cards — AP Run (outbound) and AP Pick Up (return). Both show a blue Pair badge and a button to jump to the other leg.
06
📋
My Rides & Confirmation Texts
Your personal ride list is free. The day-before confirmation workflow requires Premium.
My Rides list — Free📋 To Confirm — Premium ✦
1
My Rides — your personal schedule (Free)
Rides you created or are assigned to, in Calendar, Upcoming list, and To Confirm tabs.
2
📋 To Confirm — day before only (Premium ✦)
Shows tomorrow's rides that still need a confirmation text. Confirmations go out the day before — not at booking.
3
Six send buttons per card (Premium ✦)
Scheduling confirmation · Pickup instructions · Selfie request · Driver contact card · Driver photo card · Client phone to driver. Each turns green ✓ when tapped.
4
Mark Sent → green checkmark (Premium ✦)
Tap Mark Confirmation Text Sent — the card slides out and a ✓ appears on the calendar.
Turn it on or off
  • Go to Team → your driver card → Schedule Confirmation toggle. Turn it off if you handle confirmations differently, on to use the built-in workflow.
07
💬
Team Chat
Two group channels for coverage and updates, plus private direct messages.
Free for all drivers
1
🚗 Offerings — ride coverage
Tap Post to Offerings on any ride. A card appears — teammates tap ✅ I'll take it and are assigned instantly.
2
🚦 Updates — traffic & conditions
Share road conditions without buzzing phones. Shows in the bell but won't pop an OS notification.
3
@ Direct Messages
Tap @ to message any driver privately. DMs do send an OS notification to the recipient.
🔔 Notification rules
Offerings ✓ buzzes · Updates ✗ bell only · DMs ✓ buzzes
You only get notified about things that involve you directly.
08
👥
Client List
Your private contact book — automatically built with every ride you book.
Free for all drivers
🔒 100% Confidential — This Is Your Business
Your client list is completely private. Other drivers cannot see it. No admin can see it either — not Kat, not Morgan, not Chase. Client phone numbers are also hidden in the calendar from anyone not directly on that ride. Your book of business belongs to you.
1
Auto-added when you book
Client name and phone are added every time you save a ride — no double entry.
2
Search before booking
Find existing clients before creating a ride to avoid duplicates and see history.
3
Transferring a client
New driver can request a takeover. Original driver can release their own client directly. Admins can initiate with one other admin's approval.
09
💰
Income & Receipts
Full earnings tracking, receipt scanning, and expense logs.
Premium ✦ — requires subscription
1
Weekly / Monthly / Custom range
Gross fares · Expenses · Net take-home · Ride count · Miles driven — any date range.
2
📸 Receipt scanner
Receipts → Add Receipt → take a photo. App reads it and auto-fills amount, date, and category.
3
Monthly recurring expenses
Add car payment, insurance, phone once — they carry forward every month automatically.
4
15% Client Fee tracker
Track fees owed and receivable with one-tap Venmo payment when handles are saved.
5
Trip Profit calculator
Hamburger → Trip Profit. Exact net profit per ride after gas, maintenance, and the 15% Client Fee.
10
🚘
Your Driver Profile
Set your vehicle, availability, Venmo handle, and confirmation templates.
Free — hamburger menu → Team → your card
1
Vehicle & contact info
Year, model, email, gas price, MPG — feeds the Trip Profit calculator automatically.
2
Availability — days, dates, hours
Block weekdays, specific dates, or time windows. Warnings fire in Quick Add when a blocked driver is selected.
3
📅 Schedule Confirmation toggle
Turn on or off to control whether your rides appear in the To Confirm workflow (Premium).
4
✉️ Custom confirmation templates
Write your own text using {client}, {date}, {time}, {amount} placeholders — fills in per ride.
5
Venmo handle & payment methods
Add @venmohandle so drivers can pay fees in one tap. Toggle accepted payment methods.
11
🔔
Notifications
Smart routing — every notification takes you right to its source.
Free for all drivers
1
Tap the bell — newest first
Red badge shows unread count. Tap any item to go there — it removes itself from the list when opened.
2
Smart routing
Ride → opens that ride. Chat → opens that channel or DM. Transfer or client request → opens Clients.
3
OS pop-up notifications
Phone buzzes for: ride assigned to you · DMs · Offerings posts · approvals. Nothing else.
4
Enable push — the 📳 button
Tap 📳 in the top bar after first login to allow notifications in the background.

✈️
Flights & Live Traffic
Track flights, check TSA wait times, and see live Atlanta traffic.
Flights — FreeTraffic tab — Premium ✦
1
✈️ Flights tab — Free
Search any flight. Today's and tomorrow's AP rides with tracking links, ATL Arrivals, and FAA Status.
2
🔒 TSA wait times — Free

3
🚦 Traffic tab — Premium ✦

13 — Install
⬇️
Get the App on Your Phone
Install in 30 seconds — no app store, works on any phone.
📷 Scan to open the app

🍎 iPhone — Safari only

1

Open Safari — must be Safari, not Chrome.

2

Scan the QR code or go to the app link in Safari.

3

Tap the Share button — square with arrow at the bottom of Safari.

4

Scroll down and tap "Add to Home Screen." Tap Add.

5

Tap the NDA icon — opens full screen.

🤖 Android — Chrome

1

Open Chrome and go to the app link.

2

Tap the three dots ⋮ in the top right.

3

Tap "Add to Home Screen" or "Install App."

4

Tap Add — icon appears, opens full screen.

After installing
  • Tap 📳 in the top bar after first login to enable push notifications.
  • Updates happen automatically in the background.
