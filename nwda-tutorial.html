01
🔐
Logging In
One-tap Face ID or fingerprint after your first password login.
Free for all drivers
1
Select your name & enter password
Pick your name from the list and type your password on first use.
2
Set up Face ID or fingerprint
A prompt appears after first login. Tap Set Up — from then on it's one tap. Manage it any time from Team → your driver card → 🔐 Biometric.
3
Stay Logged In
Check this on your personal phone and the app will remember you between sessions.
First login
  • A popup will appear asking if you want to check out the tutorial. You can close it for now or permanently dismiss it — it's always available in the ☰ menu.
02
🏠
Dashboard
Your real-time home base — rides, confirmations, and earnings.
Rides — FreeEarnings & Confirmations — Premium ✦
1
Your upcoming rides (Free)
All rides for today and this week in time order. Tap any card to open the full detail.
2
📋 Confirmations due tomorrow (Premium ✦)
Orange banner shows rides needing a day-before text. Requires Premium — tap it to go to To Confirm.
3
💰 Monthly earnings snapshot (Premium ✦)
Gross fares, expenses, and net take-home for the current month.
4
15% Client Fee tracker (Premium ✦)
Shows the specific ride and amount you owe or are owed. Pay via one-tap Venmo when handles are saved, or mark paid manually.
03
📝
Quoting a Ride
Build a price, text it to the client, then book it with one tap.
Free for all drivers
1
Pick your trip type
AP Round Trip, AP Run, AP Pick Up, Round Trip, One Way, or Local. Fields adapt automatically.
2
Enter addresses — mileage fills automatically
Google Maps calculates mileage. Any miles over 50 get priced automatically.
3
Add surcharges
Late Notice · Holiday · Traffic · Fuel (rounds total down to whole dollar) · Extra Stop · Wait Time.
4
Send Quote → Book This Ride
Send Quote opens Messages with the breakdown ready. Book This Ride saves the ride and prompts a scheduling confirmation text.
04
➕
Booking a Ride
Get a ride on the calendar in under 30 seconds with Quick Add.
Free for all drivers
1
Tap ➕ to open Quick Add
From the calendar or bottom nav. Client name and date are the only required fields.
2
Client ownership warning
If the client is in another driver's list, a yellow banner appears. Tap "🔗 Request this client →" to send a transfer request without leaving the form.
3
Unavailable driver warning
If the selected driver is marked unavailable, a popup asks you to confirm before saving. If you proceed, the driver gets a notification asking them to accept or decline.
4
Save → two-step text prompt
Step 1: scheduling confirmation text. Step 2: booking policy. Both pre-written — tap Send or Skip.
AP Round Trip
  • Automatically splits into two linked rides — an AP Run and an AP Pick Up — each showing a blue 🔗 Pair badge on the calendar. Tap either to jump to the other leg.
05
📅
Calendar
Your full schedule, color-coded by driver — week or month view.
Free for all drivers
1
Week or Month view
Toggle at the top. Week shows time slots, Month shows color pills per day — tap a day to see its rides.
2
🔗 Linked AP Round Trip pairs
AP Round Trips create two linked cards — AP Run (outbound) and AP Pick Up (return). Both show a blue Pair badge. Tap either card for a "View other leg →" button.
3
🔒 Contact privacy
Client phone numbers are hidden from anyone who isn't the assigned driver or the ride creator. Admins cannot see contact info either.
4
📤 Post to Offerings
Need coverage? Tap Post to Offerings inside the ride detail — a card goes to the Offerings chat for the team to claim.
06
📋
My Rides & Confirmations
My Rides list is free. The day-before confirmation workflow requires Premium.
My Rides — Free📋 To Confirm — Premium ✦
1
My Rides — your personal schedule (Free)
Rides you created or are assigned to, in Calendar, Upcoming, and To Confirm tabs.
2
📋 To Confirm — tomorrow only (Premium ✦)
Shows rides happening tomorrow that still need a confirmation text. Confirmations go out the day before — not at booking time.
3
Six send buttons (Premium ✦)
Scheduling confirmation · Pickup instructions · Selfie request · Driver contact card · Driver photo card · Client phone to driver. Each turns green ✓ when tapped.
4
Mark Sent → green checkmark (Premium ✦)
Card slides out and a ✓ appears on the calendar next to that client's name.
07
💬
Team Chat
Two group channels for coverage and updates, plus private messages.
Free for all drivers
1
🚗 Offerings — ride coverage
Post any ride needing coverage. A card appears with client, date, time, and price. Teammates tap ✅ I'll take it and are assigned instantly.
2
🚦 Updates — traffic & conditions
Share road conditions without buzzing phones. Shows in the bell but won't pop an OS notification.
3
@ Direct Messages
Tap @ to message any driver privately. DMs do send an OS notification to the recipient.
🔔 Notification rules
Offerings ✓ buzzes · Updates ✗ bell only · DMs ✓ buzzes
You only get notified about things that involve you directly.
08
👥
Client List
Your private contact book — automatically built with every ride you book.
Free for all drivers
🔒 100% Confidential — This Is Your Business
Your client list is completely private. Other drivers cannot see it. No admin can see it either — not Kat, not Morgan, not Chase. Client phone numbers are also hidden in the calendar from anyone not directly on that ride. Your book of business belongs to you.
1
Auto-added when you book
Client name and phone are added every time you save a ride — no double entry.
2
Client ownership warning in Quick Add
If a client already belongs to another driver, a yellow warning appears. You can request that client with one tap, right from the booking form.
3
Transferring a client
The new driver requests it, the original driver releases it directly, or an admin initiates a transfer with one other admin's approval.
09
💰
Income & Receipts
Full earnings tracking, receipt scanning, and expense logs.
Premium ✦ — requires subscription
1
Weekly / Monthly / Custom range
Gross fares, expenses, net take-home, ride count — any date range you choose.
2
📸 Receipt scanner
Receipts → Add Receipt → take a photo. App reads it and auto-fills amount, date, and category.
3
15% Client Fee — per ride breakdown
Shows the exact client and date that generated each fee. The fee always goes to the CLIENT'S OWNER — not necessarily who assigned the ride.
4
🔗 Combined income (linked profiles)
If you've linked profiles with a partner driver, a toggle at the top of Income lets you see your combined totals. Off by default — both profiles always stay separate.
5
Trip Profit calculator
Hamburger → Trip Profit. Exact net profit per ride after gas, maintenance, and the 15% Client Fee.
10
🚘
Your Driver Profile
Vehicle, availability, Venmo, templates — and profile linking.
Free — hamburger menu → Team → your card
1
Vehicle & contact info
Year, model, email, gas price, MPG — feeds the Trip Profit calculator automatically.
2
Availability — days, dates, hours
Block weekdays, specific dates, or time windows. A warning fires in Quick Add when a blocked driver is selected, and the assigner must confirm before saving.
3
✉️ Custom confirmation templates
Write your own text using {client}, {date}, {time}, {amount} — fills in automatically per ride.
4
🔗 Request Profile Merge (hamburger menu)
Link with a partner driver for shared viewing of clients, rides, and income. Both profiles stay completely separate — this is read access only, with an optional combined income toggle. Admin must approve.
11
🔔
Notifications
Smart routing — every notification takes you right to its source.
Free for all drivers
1
Tap the bell — newest first
Red badge shows unread count. Tap any item to go there — it removes itself when opened.
2
Smart routing
Ride → opens that ride. Chat → opens that channel or DM. Transfer or client request → opens Clients.
3
Availability requests
If an assigner books you while you're marked unavailable, you get a notification asking to accept or decline — with a 24-hour response window before the assigner is notified of no response.
4
Enable push — the 📳 button
Tap 📳 in the top bar after first login to allow notifications in the background.
12
🆘
SOS Emergency Button
Instant team-wide emergency alert with optional location sharing.
Premium ✦ — requires subscription
1
Tap the red 🆘 button in the top bar
Only visible to Premium subscribers. Available any time you're logged in.
2
🚨 Personal Emergency — shares your location
Medical, safety, or personal crisis. The app requests your GPS location and includes a live Google Maps link in every driver's alert.
3
🚗 Ride Emergency — message only
Car trouble, accident, or client issue. Add a message describing where you are and what's happening.
4
Every driver is notified immediately
A full-screen red popup appears for each driver the moment they open the app, showing the type, message, date, time, and a map link if location was shared.
5
Auto-clears once everyone sees it
Tap "Got it — don't show again" to dismiss. Once every driver in the group has seen it, the alert clears automatically — no manual clearing needed.
Responding to an SOS
  • Any driver can tap "I can help → Notify [Name]" which immediately sends a notification back to the person who triggered the SOS so they know help is on the way.
  • The sender sees their own popup with an "I'm OK — Clear SOS" button to resolve it early.

Flights & Live Traffic

Flights — FreeTraffic tab — Premium ✦
1
✈️ Flights tab — Free
Search any flight. Today's and tomorrow's AP rides with tracking links, ATL Arrivals, and FAA Status.
2

14 — Install
⬇️
Get the App on Your Phone
Install in 30 seconds — no app store, works on any phone.
📷 Scan to open the app

🍎 iPhone — Safari only

1

Open Safari — must be Safari, not Chrome.

2

Scan the QR code or go to the app link in Safari.

3

Tap the Share button — square with arrow at the bottom of Safari.

4

Scroll down and tap "Add to Home Screen." Tap Add.

5

Tap the NDA icon on your home screen — opens full screen.

🤖 Android — Chrome

1

Open Chrome and go to the app link.

2

Tap the three dots ⋮ in the top right.

3

Tap "Add to Home Screen" or "Install App."

4

Tap Add — icon appears, opens full screen.

After installing
  • Tap 📳 in the top bar after first login to enable push notifications.
  • Find this tutorial any time in the ☰ hamburger menu → Driver Tutorial.
  • Updates happen automatically — you always have the latest version.
