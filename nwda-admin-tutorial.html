The Admin Playbook
Everything you can do that regular drivers can’t — assigning rides, managing the team, approving requests, controlling client lists, and pulling the reports that keep the business honest. This guide is admin-only; don’t share the link with drivers.
Admins only
★
Who counts as an admin
Kat, Chase, and Morgan. Admins see extra buttons and panels throughout the app that are invisible to drivers.
Tip: tap the ☰ menu (bottom-left) any time to jump straight to a topic.
Rides
Assign & Manage Any Ride
Drivers can only touch rides they created. Admins can touch every ride on the calendar.
Admin power
1
Assign a driver to any ride
Open any ride card and use the Driver dropdown → Assign. The driver gets a notification. Drivers can only assign rides they personally created — you can assign all of them.
2
Reassign or unassign
Change the driver any time. If the new driver isn’t available at that time, the app warns you and lets you override.
3
Cancel any ride
Admins can cancel any ride on the calendar, not just their own.
Editing note: the Edit button still follows creator rules — only the driver who created a ride can change its details. Assigning and cancelling are the admin-wide powers.
The Team
Manage Drivers
The Drivers page is your team control panel. Admins can edit every driver’s profile and settings.
Admin power
1
Edit any driver’s profile
Update contact info, vehicle, photo, and the home address used for mileage routing. Drivers can only edit their own; you can edit all.
2
Set availability & days off
Mark weekly days off, add unavailable hours, or block specific dates for any driver. The app uses this to warn before double-booking.
3
Enable or disable access
Disable a driver to revoke their login without deleting their history. Re-enable any time.
4
Per-driver pricing & cost settings
The pricing tracker panel (admin-only) lets you review each driver’s gas, MPG, and maintenance rates that feed their income math.
Approvals
Client Transfers & Releases
When a driver wants to hand a client to someone else — or release one back to the pool — it comes to the admins to approve.
Admin approval
1
Where requests show up
Pending transfer and release requests appear in your notification bell. Drivers don’t see each other’s requests — only admins (and the driver involved) do.
2
Approve or deny
Open the request and choose. Approving moves the client to the new driver; denying leaves things as they were and notifies the requester.
3
Admin-initiated transfers
You can also start a transfer or release yourself from a client’s page, without waiting for a driver to ask.
Client Privacy
Unlock a Driver’s Client List
Each driver’s client list is private. To view another driver’s list, an admin needs a second admin’s approval — a deliberate two-key safeguard.
Double approvalSensitive
1
Request access
On the Client Directory, tap 🔒 Unlock List, pick the driver whose list you need, and send the request. You count as the first approval automatically.
2
A different admin approves
Every other admin gets a notification. A second, different admin must approve — you can’t approve your own request.
3
24-hour access
Once approved, a dropdown lets you switch to that driver’s list. Access auto-expires after 24 hours and can be revoked any time.
Client Privacy
Contact Info Is Locked Down
Client phone numbers are some of the most sensitive data in the app. Even admins don’t get a free pass.
Privacy by design
1
Who sees a phone number
Only the ride’s creator and the assigned driver. This holds in the ride card, the calendar search, and everywhere else — being an admin does not reveal numbers on rides you’re not part of.
2
The client list unlock is the path
If you genuinely need another driver’s client contacts, that’s what the double-admin Unlock List flow is for — it’s logged and time-limited on purpose.
This protects the drivers’ books of business and keeps client trust intact. It’s a feature, not a limitation.
Reports
Pricing Audit Report
The single most useful admin report. It breaks down every ride’s price into its parts and flags anything that doesn’t match the standard formula.
Admin report
1
Where to find it
On the Income page, tap 📋 Pricing Audit (admins only). It respects the date range you’ve selected — This Week, Month, Custom, or All Time.
2
What’s in it
A CSV with each ride broken into base rate, mileage, service fee, every surcharge, manual adjustments, and the final price — plus column totals.
3
The variance column is the gold
It compares the saved price to what the standard formula should produce. Any non-zero variance flags a manual override, a legacy price, or an admin-set rate — so you can spot pricing drift instantly.
Opens in Excel, Google Sheets, or Numbers. A $0.00 variance means the ride was priced exactly by the book.
Reports
Income & Year-End P&L
Admins see income across the operation, not just their own rides, plus the tools to close out the year.
Admin view
1
Whole-operation income
On the Income page, admins see fleet-wide numbers. Drivers only ever see their own.
2
Export the full CSV
Export CSV pulls every ride’s income detail for the selected range.
3
Year-End P&L spreadsheet
📊 Year-End P&L builds a formatted Excel workbook with the full profit-and-loss for tax time.
Shared Files
Documents & Folders
The Documents area is a shared library for the whole team. Admins control its structure.
Admin power
1
Manage folders
Tap Manage Folders (admin-only) to create, rename, or color-code the categories drivers file documents under.
2
Delete any document
Admins can remove any shared document. Drivers can only delete the ones they uploaded.
Safety
The Cautious List
A shared heads-up list for clients the team should approach carefully. Admins curate it.
Team safety
1
Add an entry
Flag a client with a note so every driver sees the caution before accepting a ride.
2
Remove entries
Admins can remove any entry; drivers can only remove ones they added. Keep it current and fair.
Use this for genuine safety and reliability concerns — it’s visible to the whole team, so keep notes factual and professional.
Oversight
What Lands in Your Bell
Admins receive a wider set of notifications because more decisions route through you.
Admin view
🔔
Transfer & release requests
Every pending client transfer or release waiting on a decision.
🔔
Client-list unlock requests
When another admin asks to view a driver’s list, you’re notified so you can be the second approver.
🔔
Profile merge & override requests
Availability overrides and profile-merge requests surface to admins for a decision.
Opening the bell marks ride notifications as read. Action items like transfer requests stay until you actually approve or deny them — that’s intentional, so nothing slips through.
The Bottom Line
Admin Responsibilities
With the extra power comes the job of keeping the operation fair, accurate, and trustworthy.

✓
Respect client privacy

✓
Be fair with the team
